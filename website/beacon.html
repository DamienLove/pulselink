Highlights

  • Favorites break through DND — selected contacts can alert you audibly even in Do Not Disturb.
  • Keyphrase alarm — an incoming message with your private keyphrase triggers an audible alarm.
  • Safety interop — linked Safety alerts can trigger Beacon’s emergency siren on your device.
  • FCM delivery — when paired with PulseLink Safety, messages and signals can route via Firebase Cloud Messaging over Wi‑Fi (no SIM required).

Settings

Favorites & DND

  • Grant DND override to Beacon when prompted.
  • Settings → Manage favorites: choose who can break through DND.
  • Pick an emergency siren tone for urgent cases.

Keyphrase Alarm

  • Choose a private phrase unlikely in normal conversation.
  • When a message contains it, Beacon plays an alarm even in DND.
  • Change or disable anytime in Settings.

With PulseLink Safety

If a trusted contact triggers an emergency in Safety, Beacon can sound an emergency siren for you, helping you respond faster.

Tips

  • Keep notifications allowed for timely alerts.
  • Review permissions after OS upgrades.
  • All features are optional and can be turned off at any time.

Diagnostics

If an expected audible alert does not play, open Settings → Diagnostics → Export logs and share with support. This helps troubleshoot DND policy, notification channels, and FCM delivery.
