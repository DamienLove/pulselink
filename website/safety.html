What Safety does

  • Listens for discreet trigger phrases (wake words) to start an emergency flow.
  • Escalates alerts to your trusted contacts, including your live location.
  • Interoperates with PulseLink Beacon to sound a remote siren on linked devices.
  • Delivers link requests/approvals and messages over Wi‑Fi using Firebase Cloud Messaging (no SMS required).

Setup

  1. Add trusted contacts inside the app. Contacts are stored securely in Firebase.
  2. Set your trigger phrases and optional cancel/check‑in phrases.
  3. Allow microphone, location, and notification permissions.
  4. Optional: enable WebRTC signaling (beta) if available on your device for low‑latency sessions.

Emergency flow

  1. You trigger Safety (voice phrase or deep link). The app starts capturing the essentials.
  2. Safety composes an alert including a Google Maps link to your location.
  3. Trusted contacts receive the alert via secure FCM relay; linked Beacon clients can sound the siren.
  4. You can cancel with your cancel phrase or via the in‑app sheet (optional PIN).
When alerts include location, recipients get a map deeplink like: https://maps.google.com/?q=LAT,LNG.

Home screen widget

Add the PulseLink widget for one‑tap actions: Emergency, Cancel, and Check‑in. Long‑press the home screen → Widgets → PulseLink.

Hands‑free shortcuts

Use assistant actions or deep links for quick access. See Deep Links for all options.

Diagnostics

If delivery seems delayed, open Settings → Diagnostics → Export logs. Share logs with support to help investigate FCM delivery, permissions, or network issues.

Privacy

  • Data minimization: only what’s required for safety is sent to trusted contacts.
  • You control who can receive alerts and which phrases are active.
