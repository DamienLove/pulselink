# Beacon – Goals & Roadmap

Product goals

- Be a great default SMS/MMS experience that respects safety contexts.
- Make sure the right people can always reach you (favorites, keyphrase, interop).
- Keep detection on‑device and privacy‑preserving by default.
- Require only one party to install the app.

Near‑term

- Favorites management UI and tones.
- Keyphrase alarm with safe defaults and onboarding.
- Reliable DND override flows on Android 13–15+.
- Safety interop (receive siren when a linked contact triggers emergency in Safety).

Beacon Pro (exploratory)

- AI summarization of long threads (on‑device or private by design).
- Contextual DND bypass for emergent language indicating risk.
- App “cloner” bridges for favorite third‑party messengers where feasible and policy‑compliant.

Quality & compliance

- Strict background execution and battery optimization guidance.
- Clear disclosures for DND and notification policy access.
- Respect Play Console policies and SMS/Notifications permissions.
