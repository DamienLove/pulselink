# Beacon × Safety Integration

When you link contacts in PulseLink Safety, Beacon can participate in the alert flow so you don’t miss urgent events:

- If a linked contact triggers an emergency in Safety, Beacon can play the emergency siren on your device.
- If you set a keyphrase in Beacon, regular SMS messages containing that phrase will trigger an alarm on your device — even if the sender doesn’t use Beacon.

Setup

1. In Beacon → Settings, enable “Safety interop”.
2. In Safety, ensure the contact is linked and you’ve granted DND override.
3. Optionally set your emergency siren tone in Beacon.

Notes

- On Android 15+, verify the “Emergency Alerts” notification channel can bypass DND.
- Beacon will only play sirens when you’ve explicitly enabled the feature and granted the necessary permissions.
