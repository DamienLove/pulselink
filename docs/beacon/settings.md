# Beacon Settings

Core settings

- Favorites can break through DND
  - Let selected contacts alert you with a distinct tone even when your phone is on Do Not Disturb.
  - Manage favorites from Settings → Manage favorites.
- Keyphrase alarm
  - When an incoming message contains your private keyphrase, Beacon plays an audible alarm.
  - Choose a phrase unlikely to occur in normal conversation.
- Notification policy (DND)
  - Grant DND override so Beacon can deliver audible alerts when needed.
  - On Android 15+, confirm the “Emergency Alerts” channel can bypass DND.
- Safety interop
  - If a PulseLink Safety‑linked contact triggers an emergency, Beacon sounds the emergency siren for you.
- Emergency siren tone
  - Pick the sound that plays for emergencies.

Tips

- You can keep Settings concise and read detailed guidance in the in‑app Help (top‑right Help icon).
- All features are optional and can be turned off at any time.
