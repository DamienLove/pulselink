PulseLink Beacon is a safety‑aware SMS/MMS app that helps you hear the right messages at the right time — even through Do Not Disturb.

What makes Beacon different

- Favorites can always reach you: Pick the people who get a special tone and can break through DND.
- Keyphrase alarm: Set a private word or phrase; when a message contains it, Beacon plays an audible alarm so you don’t miss it. The sender doesn’t need Beacon installed.
- Works with PulseLink Safety: If a trusted, linked contact triggers an emergency in the Safety app, Beacon can play the emergency siren on your phone too.
- Private by design: Detection happens on your device. You control permissions and can turn features on or off anytime.

Why it matters

Important messages can be buried by noise. Beacon makes sure the people who matter — and the messages that matter — are heard when you need them, without asking everyone to switch apps.

Key features

- Favorites that break through DND with a distinct tone
- Keyphrase alarm for urgent texts
- Emergency siren tone selection
- PulseLink Safety interop to hear emergencies from trusted contacts
- Clear setup for DND and notification policies

Privacy & permissions

- Notifications/SMS access is used to detect favorites and keyphrase matches.
- Do Not Disturb access enables audible alerts when you’ve allowed it.
- Content analysis happens on‑device; message content is not uploaded for these features.

Beacon Pro (roadmap)

- Optional AI summarization of long threads
- Contextual DND bypass when messages indicate emergent risk

Disclaimers

- Beacon is not a substitute for emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (such as 911).
- Behavior may vary by device model and Android version. On Android 15+, ensure the “Emergency Alerts” channel can bypass DND in system settings.
