# PulseLink Safety

PulseLink Safety is focused on emergencies. It listens for discreet trigger phrases and escalates alerts with your location to your trusted contacts.

Key capabilities

- Discreet trigger phrases to start an emergency.
- Shares your latest location in alerts (optional).
- Do Not Disturb override so sirens and critical alerts are heard.
- Linked contacts for rapid, private outreach.
- Custom alert tones for emergencies, check‑ins, and urgent calls.

Settings and help

- The Settings screen keeps labels short and actionable.
- For detailed explanations and troubleshooting, open the in‑app Settings Help from the top‑right Help icon.

Works with Beacon

- If a trusted contact uses Beacon, their device can play the emergency siren when you trigger an alert, helping them hear you even through DND.
