1. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information to provide and improve our Service. The information we collect includes:

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In or other authentication methods, we collect your name, email address, and profile information from your authentication provider.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us for support, we collect your email address and any information you provide in your communications.
  • Payment Information: When you purchase a subscription or in-app purchase, payment information is processed by Google Play's billing system. We do not store your payment card details, but we receive transaction information (subscription status, purchase date) from Google Play.

1.2 SMS and MMS Data

SoText functions as an SMS/MMS messaging application. To provide core messaging features, we access and process:

  • Message Content: Text messages (SMS) and multimedia messages (MMS) you send and receive, including message bodies, attachments, timestamps, and delivery status.
  • Message Metadata: Phone numbers of senders and recipients, conversation threads, read/unread status, and message categories.
  • Message Analysis: We use on-device AI and cloud-based AI (Firebase AI) to analyze message content for emergency detection, spam filtering, and categorization features. This analysis helps identify urgent or emergency messages to trigger appropriate alerts.
  • SoText Inbox Feature: Messages categorized as "SoText Inbox" messages (check-ins, emergency alerts) may be processed differently to provide safety and emergency response features.

Storage: Messages are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database. If you enable optional sync features or remote web access, message data may be synchronized to Firebase Firestore (cloud storage) to enable cross-device sync and remote access via web browser.

1.3 Contact Information

  • When you grant permission, we access your device contacts to display contact names, phone numbers, and profile pictures in conversations.
  • Contact information is used locally on your device and may be synced to the cloud if you enable sync features.

1.4 Location Data

  • Precise Location: We collect your precise location (GPS coordinates) when you use location-sharing features, emergency alert features, or enable location-based services.
  • Location Sharing: You can share your location with specific contacts through messages. Location data is transmitted directly to the recipient and may be stored in message history.
  • Emergency Location: When you trigger emergency alerts or use emergency features, your location is collected and may be shared with your designated emergency contacts.

1.5 Phone and Call Data

  • Phone State: We access your phone's state to detect incoming calls and SMS for caller ID features.
  • Phone Numbers: We read your phone number and SIM card information to configure messaging functionality.
  • Call Log: We access call log data to provide caller ID information and integrate calling features with messaging.
  • Caller ID Lookups: When enabled, phone numbers from incoming calls or messages may be sent to third-party caller ID services (see Section 4) to retrieve caller information such as name, location, and spam risk.

1.6 Device Information

  • Device Identifiers: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information.
  • App Configuration: Your app settings, preferences, theme selections, notification preferences, and feature configurations.
  • Installed Apps: We check for the presence of specific apps (Google Assistant, Bard) to enable assistant integration features.

1.7 Usage and Analytics Data

  • App Usage: Features you use, screens you view, actions you take, frequency and duration of use.
  • Performance Data: App crashes, errors, performance metrics, and diagnostic information collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Interaction Data: Button clicks, navigation paths, and user interactions within the app.

1.8 Advertising Data (Free Tier Only)

If you use SoText Free (standard tier), we collect and share data with advertising partners:

  • Advertising ID: Your Google Advertising ID for personalized advertising.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and conversions.
  • Ad Partners: We use Google AdMob and mediation partners including AppLovin, InMobi, IronSource, Meta (Facebook), Pangle, and Vungle to serve advertisements.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

  • Device Database: Messages, contacts, and app data are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database using Android Room.
  • Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted using Android's Security Crypto library and DataStore with encryption at rest.
  • App Data: Preferences, settings, and configurations are stored in encrypted DataStore on your device.

3.2 Cloud Storage (Optional)

  • Firebase Firestore: If you enable sync features or remote web access, your messages, contacts, settings, and themes may be synchronized to Firebase Firestore, a cloud database operated by Google.
  • Firebase Storage: Media attachments (images, videos, audio) may be stored in Firebase Cloud Storage.
  • Data Location: Cloud data is stored on Google's servers, primarily in the United States (us-central1 region).
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted to and from cloud services uses TLS/SSL encryption.

3.3 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption of data at rest and in transit
  • Secure authentication using Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication
  • Access controls and permission management
  • Regular security updates and vulnerability patching
  • Firebase Security Rules to protect cloud data access
  • Play Integrity API to prevent tampering and unauthorized access

Important: While we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, authentication credentials, and preventing unauthorized access to your device.

4.2 Caller ID Services (When Enabled)

When you use caller ID features, phone numbers may be sent to these third-party lookup services:

  • NumLookup API: Phone number validation and caller information
  • Numverify: Phone number verification and carrier lookup
  • IPQualityScore: Spam risk assessment and fraud detection
  • Twilio Lookup: Carrier information and number validation
  • Truecaller (via RapidAPI): Caller name and spam identification
  • RealCall.ai: Spam and scam caller identification
  • Phone Number Lookup API (RapidAPI): General phone number information

Data Sent: Phone numbers only (no message content or personal information is sent to caller ID services).

Rate Limits: Lookups are subject to monthly caps to protect your privacy and control costs.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

5.1 Retention Periods

  • Message Data: Stored locally on your device until you delete conversations or uninstall the app. Cloud-synced messages are retained until you disable sync or delete your account.
  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after account deletion to comply with legal obligations.
  • Analytics and Logs: Aggregated analytics data is retained indefinitely. Individual logs and crash reports are retained for 90 days.
  • Payment Records: Transaction records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations.

5.2 Your Deletion Rights

You have the right to delete your data:

  • Delete Messages: Delete individual messages or entire conversations within the app at any time.
  • Clear App Data: Clear all app data from your device through Android Settings > Apps > SoText > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Disable Sync: Turn off cloud sync in app settings to stop syncing data to Firebase. Previously synced data will remain in the cloud until you request deletion.
  • Delete Cloud Data: Contact us at support@sotext.app to request deletion of all cloud-stored data.
  • Delete Account: You can delete your account and associated data by going to remove.damiennichols.com or you can request account deletion by emailing support@sotext.app. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.
  • Uninstall App: Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device but does not delete cloud-synced data or your account.

5.3 Retention After Deletion

After you delete data or your account:

  • Backup copies may be retained for up to 30 days for disaster recovery purposes
  • Some data may be retained in aggregated, anonymized form for analytics
  • Legal obligations may require retention of certain records (e.g., payment transactions)
  • Cached data on third-party services may persist for a short period

6.2 Permission Controls

You can control app permissions through Android Settings > Apps > SoText > Permissions:

  • SMS: Required for core messaging functionality. Denying this permission will prevent the app from functioning as an SMS app.
  • Contacts: Optional. Deny to prevent contact name display.
  • Location: Optional. Deny to prevent location sharing and emergency location features.
  • Phone: Optional. Deny to prevent caller ID features.
  • Notifications: Optional. Deny to prevent push notifications.

6.3 Feature Controls

Control data collection through in-app settings:

  • Cloud Sync: Disable to prevent message syncing to Firebase.
  • Remote Web Access: Disable to prevent remote access to your messages via web browser.
  • Caller ID: Disable to prevent phone number lookups to third-party services.
  • AI Message Analysis: Disable emergency detection to prevent message content analysis (note: this disables emergency features).
  • Location Sharing: Control whether to share location in messages.

6.4 Advertising Choices (Free Tier)

  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Go to Android Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Go to Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.

6.5 Marketing Communications

  • You can opt out of promotional emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • You can disable push notifications in app settings or Android system settings.
