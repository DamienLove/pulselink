RingerSong status (last updated 2025-12-27)

Overview
- Android app plays progressive ringtone segments from local audio files or Spotify.
- **NEW (v3.2):** Full Spotify integration via Spotify App Remote and Web API.
- **NEW (v3.2):** Cross-platform sync with PulseLink WebApp via Firebase Firestore (ringer_playlist).
- Per-contact assignments with urgency ringer.
- Shuffle/ordered playlist, adjustable segment length and max songs.
- Zedge convenience button.
- YouTube Support: Users can paste a YouTube URL to download audio directly as an MP3 ringer.

Integration
- **Spotify**: Users can search tracks (Web API) and play them via Spotify App Remote.
- **Firebase**: App syncs playlist with Firestore `users/{uid}/ringer_playlist`.
- **Web Companion**: PulseLink WebApp now has a RingerSong tab to manage the playlist from the browser.
